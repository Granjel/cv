# Part B

**Scientific profile:** I am a community ecologist using experiments and models to study how species interactions shape coexistence and recovery after disturbance. I have published **8 journal articles** (3 as first and corresponding author) and **1 book chapter**, with **101 citations** (WoS). My **6.5 years of research outside my doctoral institution** include stays abroad in **5 countries** (20 months during my PhD and 7.5 as a postdoc).

**PhD research:** During my FPU-funded PhD in Spain with Oscar Godoy and Juan Arroyo, I led studies with colleagues in Switzerland and France, combining structural stability theory with field data to test **plant coexistence beyond pairwise interactions**. I graduated *cum laude* with an International Mention.
- **IRNAS-CSIC** (Spain, Lorena Gómez-Aparicio’s lab; 8 months, 2017–18): I surveyed Doñana marsh plants and continued later through MeDiNaS (C.3#5). I am now writing a manuscript on temporal niche variability using the full 2015–24 series.
- **Universität Bern** (Switzerland, Eric Allan’s lab; 5 months, 2017–19): I generated new plant-interaction data on the local PaNDiv experiment, helped with its maintenance, and gave an invited seminar. My data showed that nutrient and pathogen effects on coexistence depend on species number (Granjel *et al.* 2023 *New Phytol.*; C.1#6). Our follow-up on land-use intensification in 150 German grasslands (Biodiversity Exploratories) is under review at *J. Ecol.* (C.1#10).
- With colleagues at **INRAE (France)**, I led the analysis and developed the code showing how plant competition and facilitation help explain coexistence under grasshopper herbivory (Granjel *et al.* 2026 *Oikos*; C.1#1).

**Independence:** During my PhD, I secured **4 additional competitive grants and fellowships (>€30k)** for work outside my PhD group, including **2 projects I conceived and led**.
- **UNESP** (Brazil, Tadeu Siqueira’s lab; 5 months, 2019–20; **Santander grant**, €5k): I shared my coexistence expertise and learnt freshwater metacommunity ecology from the team. Two schools in São Paulo gave me 130 hours of training in mathematical biology and community ecology. I conceived the idea for a manuscript on plankton coexistence under agrochemical stress. I now coordinate the UNESP–BC3 team preparing it and am its last and corresponding author.
- **MBG-CSIC** (Spain, Xoaquín Moreira’s lab; 8 months in 2 stays, 2019 and 2021; **FPU mobility grant**, €1.6k): I secured **AEET funding as PI** (€2k; C.3#2) and designed and led experiments showing that volatile signalling increases herbivory resistance in *Arabidopsis* (Granjel *et al.* 2026 *EEB*; C.1#2). I supervised students and helped run the host lab’s experiments on plant signalling under fungal infection (Moreira *et al.* 2021 *PCE*; C.1#7).
- **NC State University** (USA, Will Petry’s lab; 10 months, 2022–23; **Fulbright fellowship**, ≈€21.6k): For a project I conceived and led on microbial interactions, coexistence and functioning (C.3#1), I set up protist experiments and optimised methods the lab still uses. I learnt **Bayesian population modelling** and community assembly using invasion graphs. We are finalising 2 joint manuscripts.

**Postdoctoral work:** At BC3, I use Bayesian invasion graphs learnt at NC State to predict **plant recovery after disturbance** under warming in Daniel Montoya’s ERC Consolidator Grant RECODYN. I worked at the Metatron with Julien Côté’s team (6 months at **CNRS SETE**, France; 2023–25). Through ChEMISt (C.3#3), I bring my MBG-CSIC work on chemical signalling and herbivory to the same experiment. I initiated a collaboration with Jürg Spaak on uncertainty in recovery predictions (1.5 months at **RPTU**, Germany; 2026). I contributed analyses of how diversity stabilises grassland productivity across scales (Huang *et al.* 2026 *Nat. Commun.*; C.1#3) and co-authored a perspective on interpreting complex experiments (*PLOS Clim.* 2025; C.1#4). Several joint studies are in preparation, 1 is under review at *J. Ecol.*, and I’m preparing **2 first-author manuscripts**.
