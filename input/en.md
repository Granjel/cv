# Part B

**Scientific profile:** I am a community ecologist studying species interactions, coexistence and ecosystem responses to environmental change. I have co-authored **8 journal publications** since 2020 (**3 as first and corresponding author**), including papers in *New Phytologist* and *Nature Communications*, and **1 book chapter**. My work has received **101 citations** to date (WoS), and I have undertaken approximately **3 years and 7 months of research stays** (27 months between Switzerland, Brazil, the USA, France and Germany; and 16 months between IRNAS-CSIC and MBG-CSIC).

**PhD research:** My FPU-funded PhD at U. Sevilla with Oscar Godoy and Juan Arroyo combined structural stability theory with field data to test **plant coexistence beyond pairwise interactions**.
- I did fieldwork in Doñana's marshlands (Caracoles Ranch) during an **8-month stay at IRNAS-CSIC** and later through MeDiNaS; I am leading a paper on temporal niche variability using 2015–2023 data.
- During **5 months of stays at Universität Bern** (Switzerland) with Eric Allan, I **produced a new dataset on plant interactions**, helped maintain the local biodiversity experiment, and gave an invited seminar. We published a paper showing that nutrient and pathogen effects on coexistence depend on the number of interacting species (*New Phytologist*, 2023).
- I continued my work with Eric Allan to link land-use intensification, coexistence and diversity loss in the German **Biodiversity Exploratories** (preprint under review at *Journal of Ecology*).
- With Nicolas Gross (INRAE, France), I quantified how plant **competition and facilitation** patterns relate to multispecies coexistence mechanisms in a diverse, multitrophic grassland (*Oikos*, 2026).

**Independent projects:** I secured competitive funding for **collaborations outside my PhD group**.
- Santander Iberoamérica funded **5 months at UNESP (Brazil)** studying freshwater metacommunities. Although COVID-19 delayed data acquisition, I have developed the questions and coordinated the team for a manuscript on plankton coexistence under agrochemical stress in which I will supervise analyses and writing as **last and corresponding author**.
- I led an **AEET-funded project** on plant chemical communication. During **2 stays at MBG-CSIC totalling 8 months** (4 funded by a competitive FPU mobility grant), my experiments showed that volatile communication increases herbivory resistance in two contrasting *Arabidopsis thaliana* populations (*Environmental and Experimental Botany*, 2026).
- During my **predoctoral Fulbright fellowship** at North Carolina State University (**10 months**), I helped establish protist experiments in Will Petry’s new laboratory for a project I conceived and led on microbial interactions, coexistence and functioning. I gained laboratory experience and learnt **Bayesian population modelling** and community assembly using **invasion graphs**, which I now apply in my postdoc. We have two joint manuscripts nearing submission.

**Postdoctoral work:** Within Daniel Montoya’s ERC Consolidator RECODYN project at BC3, I extend my research from community persistence to **recovery after disturbance**, using Bayesian models to **infer plant growth and interactions** and predict recovery under warming. I worked with local teams at CNRS SETE (Moulis, France; **approximately 6 months across 2023–2025**) using the Metatron facility. My postdoc has also opened opportunities for international collaboration: I contributed analyses and writing to a study of plant diversity and productivity stability across 235 grasslands (*Nature Communications*, 2026), and worked with mathematician Jürg Spaak at RPTU (Germany; **1.5 months**) to connect interaction estimates to assembly and recovery predictions, incorporating uncertainty. Two first-author manuscripts are currently in preparation.

**Training, scientific service and outreach:**
- I have **supervised 2 MSc theses at EHU**, one completed (manuscript nearing submission), one ongoing; and **2 international undergraduates** on research stays at MBG-CSIC, one of whom went on to complete an LMU München **BSc thesis** under my supervision that led to a Q1 paper on plant chemical communication under fungal infection (*Plant, Cell & Environment*, 2021).
- I have taught **180 hours** in the undergraduate Biology and Agricultural Engineering programmes at the Universidad de Sevilla, and **12 hours** in EHU’s Biodiversity MSc programme.
- I am a **PCI Ecology recommender**, have **reviewed 9 manuscripts** for 7 journals (e.g. *Ecological Monographs*) and served as an evaluator in 2 Fulbright calls and 1 AEET call.
- I have **organised an international workshop** on ecological stability and **served on the scientific committee** and as **session coordinator** at SIBECOL–AEET 2025.
- I organised my group’s **European Researchers’ Night** activities on human impacts on biodiversity for both children and adults in downtown Bilbao, among other outreach actions.
- For every published paper I led, I **archived data and reproducible code** in Zenodo and Dryad.
