# Part B

**Scientific profile:** I am a community ecologist studying species interactions, coexistence and ecosystem responses to environmental change. I have published **8 journal articles** (3 as first and corresponding author) and **1 book chapter**, receiving **101 citations** (WoS). I have **6 years and 6 months** of experience in research centres outside my doctoral institution, including **2 years and 3 months abroad across 5 countries**.

**PhD research:** During my FPU-funded PhD with Oscar Godoy and Juan Arroyo, I combined structural stability theory with field data to test **plant coexistence beyond pairwise interactions**. I developed this work through research stays and collaborations:
- **IRNAS-CSIC** (Spain, Lorena Gómez-Aparicio's lab; **8 months**, 2017–18): I monitored Doñana’s marshland plant dynamics, and continued later with MeDiNaS (see section C.3#5). Outputs: **1 manuscript in preparation** on niche variability using time series data (2015–2024).
- **Universität Bern** (Switzerland, Eric Allan's lab; total: **5 months**, 2017–19): I generated new plant-interaction data on the local PaNDiv experiment, helped with its maintenance, and gave an invited seminar. Outputs: **2 papers**; one showing that nutrient and pathogen effects on coexistence depend on species number (Granjel *et al.* 2023 *New Phytol.*; C.1#6), and one under review at *J. Ecol.* (C.1#10) using Biodiversity Exploratories data to study diversity loss mechanisms under land-use change.
- **Collaborating with colleagues from INRAE (France)**, I modelled plant–herbivore interactions and linked competition–facilitation patterns to multispecies coexistence (Granjel *et al.* 2026 *Oikos*; C.1#1).

**Independence:** I secured competitive funding (total: >€30k) for collaborations outside my PhD group:
- **UNESP** (Brazil, Tadeu Siqueira's lab; **5 months**, 2019–20; **Santander grant**: €5k): I shared my coexistence expertise and learnt metacommunity ecology from the host team. Output: **1 manuscript in preparation** (delayed by COVID-19) as last and corresponding author, on plankton coexistence under agrochemical stress, that I devised and coordinate between UNESP and BC3.
- **MBG-CSIC** (Spain, Xoaquín Moreira's lab; **8 months** in 2 stays, 2019 & 2021; **FPU mobility grant**: €1.6k): I helped the host team with experiments, discussions and student supervision, and I led my **AEET-funded project** (€2k; C.3#2). Outputs: **2 published papers**; one showing that volatile communication increases herbivory resistance in *Arabidopsis* (Granjel *et al.* 2026 *EEB*; C.1#2), and one on plant chemical communication under fungal infection (Moreira *et al.* 2021 *PCE*; C.1#7).
- **NC State University** (USA, Will Petry's lab; **10 months**, 2022–23; **Fulbright predoc fellowship**: ≈€21.6k): For a project I conceived and led on microbial interactions, coexistence and functioning (C.3#1), I established new protist experiments and optimised laboratory methods that the host group uses to this day. I learnt **Bayesian population modelling** and community assembly using **invasion graphs**, which I now apply in my postdoc. Output: **2 joint manuscripts nearing submission**.

**Postdoctoral work:** In Daniel Montoya’s ERC Consolidator RECODYN project at BC3, I extend my research from community persistence to **recovery after disturbance**, using Bayesian models of plant growth and interactions to predict recovery under warming. I spent **6 months at CNRS SETE** (France, Julien Côté's lab; 2023–25) using Metatron, and **1.5 months at RPTU** (Germany, Jürg Spaak's lab; 2026), doing theoretical work on uncertainty in recovery predictions. Outputs: we secured the ChEMISt project (C.3#3) to apply chemical ecology to recovery; I have **co-authored three papers**: one as 2nd author on diversity–productivity stability across grasslands (Huang *et al.* 2026 *Nat. Commun.*; C.1#3), one perspective on experimental complexity and interpretability (*PLOS Clim.* 2025; C.1#4), and one under review at *J. Ecol.* on plant community recovery; and I have **2 first-author manuscripts in preparation**.
